💸 How to Avoid Hidden Hotel Fees

You see one price online, but the final bill is higher. Hidden hotel fees can add 20–30% to your stay. Here’s how to spot them before you book.

Resort fees

Many hotels charge a mandatory “resort fee” of $10–50 per night, even if you don’t use the pool or gym. It’s often hidden until checkout. Always check the fine print or call the hotel directly before booking.

Parking fees

If you’re driving, parking can be $20–40 per night. Some hotels list it as “free,” but then charge for valet or premium spaces. Compare nearby public parking or choose a hotel with genuinely free parking.

Wi-Fi charges

Some hotels still charge for Wi-Fi, especially in resorts. Look for “free Wi-Fi” in the booking details. If it’s not mentioned, assume it’s paid or only available in the lobby.

Early check-in / late checkout

Arriving before 3 PM? You might be charged $30–50 for early check-in. Late checkout can also cost extra. Ask politely at the front desk — sometimes they’ll waive it if you’re nice.

Mini-bar and incidentals

Even if you don’t touch the mini-bar, some hotels place a hold on your card for incidentals. This hold can take days to release. Pay attention to the hold amount and ask to remove it if you don’t plan to use the services.
