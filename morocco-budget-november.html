🇲🇦 How to Explore Morocco on a Budget in November

November is one of the best months to visit Morocco: mild weather, fewer crowds, and lower prices. Here’s how to travel cheap and still see everything.

Why November is perfect

Summer heat is gone, winter rains haven’t started. Days are warm (18–24°C), nights are cool. Prices on riads and tours drop by 30–50% compared to October or December.

Cheap flights and transport

Fly into Marrakech or Agadir with low-cost carriers. Between cities, use buses (CTM, Supratours) and shared grand taxis. A bus from Marrakech to Essaouira costs around 80 MAD (€7).

Affordable riads and guesthouses

Skip luxury riads in the medina center. Look for guesthouses in the outskirts or in smaller towns. A clean double room with breakfast can cost €20–30 per night.

Eat like a local

Street food is safe and delicious: tagine, couscous, msemen, and mint tea. A full meal in a local restaurant costs €3–5. Avoid restaurants with menus in five languages.

Free and cheap activities

Hiking in the Atlas Mountains, exploring souks, watching sunset over the Sahara — all free or nearly free. Guided tours in Marrakech start at €10 if you book locally, not through your hotel.
