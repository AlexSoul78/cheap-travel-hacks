🛡️ Travel Insurance Claim Denied: What to Do Next

You bought insurance, something went wrong, and now the company refuses to pay. Don't panic. Here’s how to fight back and win.

Read the denial letter carefully

Insurance companies must state the exact reason for denial. Look for the clause they cite. Sometimes it’s a misunderstanding, not a final decision.

Check if you have grounds to appeal

Common reasons for denial: pre-existing conditions, alcohol-related incidents, extreme sports not covered, or missing documents. If any of these don’t apply, you have a strong case.

Gather all evidence

Collect medical reports, receipts, police reports, photos, and correspondence. The more proof you have, the harder it is to reject your claim.

File a formal appeal

Write a clear letter referencing your policy number, the reason for the claim, and why the denial is incorrect. Attach evidence and send it via email and registered mail.

Escalate to regulators or ombudsman

If the appeal fails, contact the insurance ombudsman in your country or the country where the policy was issued. In many cases, this forces the company to reconsider.
