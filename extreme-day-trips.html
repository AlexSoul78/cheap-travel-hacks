✈️ Extreme Day Trips: How to Fly to Another Country and Back in One Day

Forget long weekends. In 2026, travelers are flying to another country in the morning and sleeping in their own bed at night. Here's how they do it — and how you can too.

The trend: 62% growth in same-day international searches

Searches for international flights departing and returning on the same day have jumped 62% year-on-year. Travelers are realising that a single day in a new city is enough for a museum, a meal, and a walk — and it costs less than a weekend hotel stay.

Real example: London to New York and back in one day

One traveler flew from London to New York in the morning, visited the Museum of Modern Art, had lunch, walked through Central Park, and was back in London by midnight. Total time on the ground: 8 hours. The trick: early morning departure, late evening return.

How to plan an extreme day trip: step by step

  • Pick a destination within 2–3 hours of flying time. Longer flights leave too little time on the ground.
  • Book the earliest outbound and the latest inbound flight. Budget airlines often have cheap early/late fares.
  • Travel with hand luggage only. No waiting at baggage claim — you walk straight out.
  • Plan around one neighbourhood. Don't try to see the whole city. Pick a district and explore it properly.
  • Pre-book tickets for one attraction. Avoid queues and save time.

What it costs: $50–150 total

A same-day return flight on a low-cost carrier can cost as little as $50–100. Add a museum ticket ($15–25), lunch ($10–20), and public transport ($5–10). Total: $80–150 for a full day in another country. A weekend trip would cost 3–4 times more with a hotel.

Best routes for extreme day trips

  • London → Paris: 1h 15m flight, from £30 return.
  • London → Amsterdam: 1h 5m, from £25 return.
  • New York → Boston: 1h 15m, from $60 return.
  • Singapore → Kuala Lumpur: 1h, from $40 return.
  • Vienna → Venice: 1h 15m, from €25 return.
