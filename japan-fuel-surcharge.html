✈️ Why Flights via Japan Just Got More Expensive (And How to Avoid It)

Japan Airlines and ANA have raised fuel surcharges to record levels. If you're connecting through Tokyo, you're paying more. Here's what happened and how to find cheaper alternatives.

What happened

In June 2026, JAL and ANA raised fuel surcharges on international flights to Europe and North America to record highs. The surcharge for a one-way flight from Japan to Europe or North America jumped to roughly 55,000 yen (about $346) for ANA and 50,000 yen for JAL [citation:1]. In July and August, it went even higher — around 65,000 yen, or roughly $406 [citation:14].

Why it matters for your trip

If you're flying from the US or Europe to Southeast Asia, Australia, or New Zealand, you might connect through Tokyo or Osaka on JAL or ANA. That "cheap" connection is now hundreds of dollars more expensive. The surcharge is added to the base fare, so the final ticket price can easily be 20–40% higher than before.

What you can do about it

1. **Avoid routing through Japan.** Connect through Hong Kong, Singapore, Seoul, or Taipei instead. These hubs are served by carriers that haven't raised surcharges as dramatically.

2. **Fly with non-Japanese carriers.** Korean Air, Asiana, Singapore Airlines, Cathay Pacific, and EVA Air all offer competitive fares without the JAL/ANA surcharge.

3. **Use a flight search tool that shows the all-in price.** Fuel surcharges are included in the final price on most aggregators, but you need to compare routes carefully. Skyscanner and Google Flights both show the total cost [citation:3][citation:6].

4. **Check alternative departure cities.** Sometimes flying from a different US or European city and connecting through a non-Japanese hub is cheaper than the Japanese connection.

Which hubs are cheaper?

Based on current surcharge levels, here are the best alternatives for US–Asia and Europe–Asia routes:

• **Seoul (ICN):** Korean Air and Asiana often have lower surcharges than JAL/ANA.

• **Hong Kong (HKG):** Cathay Pacific is a strong option, especially for Europe–Asia.

• **Singapore (SIN):** Singapore Airlines is premium but often competitive on total price.

• **Taipei (TPE):** EVA Air is known for good value.
