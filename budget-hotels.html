🏨 Budget Hotels: How to Find Cheap Stays

Don't overpay for a place to sleep. Use these tips to find clean, comfortable, and affordable accommodation on every trip.

1. Book directly with the property

Many guesthouses and small hotels offer a lower rate if you contact them by WhatsApp or email instead of booking through an aggregator. You also avoid service fees and can negotiate a better price for longer stays.

2. Stay in guesthouses or hostels

Family-run guesthouses are often cheaper than hotels and offer a more personal experience. Hostels now have private rooms that rival budget hotels but cost much less.

3. Use price comparison sites wisely

Check several platforms (Booking, Agoda, Hostelworld, direct site) before choosing. Prices can vary by 10–20% for the same room. Clear cookies or use incognito mode to avoid price hikes.

4. Travel in shoulder season

Accommodation prices drop significantly just before or after peak season. You get the same experience with fewer crowds and lower rates.

5. Consider alternative options

Apartments, homestays, and even housesitting can be much cheaper than hotels, especially for longer stays. Platforms like Airbnb, Vrbo, or local Facebook groups often have deals.
