Packing Tips: Travel Light & Smart

Overpacking costs money, time, and energy. These simple tips will help you pack lighter, avoid baggage fees, and travel more comfortably on any trip.

1. Choose the right bag
A soft-sided bag or backpack is more flexible than a hard suitcase. It squeezes into tight overhead bins and often fits airline sizers. Always check your airline's exact dimensions before packing.
2. Use packing cubes
Packing cubes keep clothes organized and compressed. They help you fit more into a small space and find things without unpacking everything.
3. Roll, don't fold
Rolling clothes saves space and prevents wrinkles. Roll t-shirts, pants, and even jackets. Place heavy items at the bottom and light items on top.
4. Wear your bulkiest items
Travel in your heaviest shoes, jacket, and sweater. This frees up space and weight in your bag. Layer up if needed — you can always take things off during the flight.
5. Pack multi-purpose clothing
Choose neutral colors that mix and match. One pair of comfortable walking shoes can replace three pairs. Bring items that work for both day and evening.
6. Limit toiletries
Use travel-sized containers or solid shampoo bars. Most hotels provide basics, and you can buy extras on arrival. Remember the 100ml rule for liquids in carry-on.
7. Leave room for souvenirs
Don't pack your bag completely full. Leave a little space for things you might buy. A foldable tote bag is great for extra items on the way back.
