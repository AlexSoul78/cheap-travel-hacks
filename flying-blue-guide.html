✈️ Flying Blue: The Best Airline Loyalty Program for Cheap Europe Flights

Flying Blue (Air France-KLM) has been named the best airline loyalty program in the world for two years running. Here’s why it’s a game-changer for budget travelers to Europe.

Why Flying Blue stands out

Unlike most programs, Flying Blue lets you earn miles without ever flying Air France or KLM. With 19 partners including Qantas, Japan Airlines, and Etihad, you can accumulate miles through everyday spending and partner bookings. Award flights within Europe start at just 5,625 miles.

How to earn miles without flying

Transfer points from major bank programs like Chase Ultimate Rewards, Amex Membership Rewards, or Capital One. Use airline shopping portals to earn miles on purchases you already make. Link a dining program to earn miles at restaurants. Book hotels, car rentals, and even Airbnb stays through partner portals to stack miles.

Redemption value

Flying Blue miles are valued at approximately 1.2–1.5 cents per mile. A round-trip flight within Europe can cost as little as 11,250 miles plus taxes. For transatlantic flights, award tickets start around 25,000 miles one-way in economy.

Best strategies for budget travelers

1. Focus on transferable points cards (Chase Sapphire, Amex Gold) to build a flexible balance. 2. Watch for transfer bonuses (often 25–50%) from bank programs to Flying Blue. 3. Book award flights during low season for the best availability. 4. Use Seats.aero to search award availability across programs.

Common mistakes to avoid

Don’t transfer points speculatively — always confirm award availability before transferring. Watch for fuel surcharges on some partners (Air France flights often have high fees). Miles expire after 24 months of inactivity, so earn or redeem at least once every two years.
