🎃 How to Find Cheap Accommodation for Halloween in Europe

Halloween is a great time to explore Europe — atmospheric streets, autumn colors, and festive energy. But hotels can be pricey. Here’s how to save on stays.

Best affordable cities for Halloween

Instead of London or Paris, consider Prague, Budapest, Krakow, or Edinburgh. These cities offer stunning old-town vibes, plenty of Halloween events, and much lower accommodation prices.

Book early, but not too early

The best window is 4–8 weeks before Halloween. Last-minute bookings often cost more, especially on weekends. Use price calendars and set alerts for your dates.

Choose apartments over hotels

Apartments with kitchens can be cheaper than hotels, especially for 3+ nights. You also save on breakfast and dinners. Look for options on local rental sites and compare with Booking and Airbnb.

Avoid tourist hotspots

Stay one or two metro stops away from the main square. Prices drop significantly, and you still reach events in 10–15 minutes. Use maps to check the walking distance before booking.

Use cashback and loyalty programs

Cashback services and bank travel rewards can return 3–7% on hotel bookings. Combine with seasonal promo codes for maximum savings.
