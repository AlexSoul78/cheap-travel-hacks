🚆 How to Travel Europe by Train on a Budget

Trains are the most scenic way to explore Europe — and they don't have to be expensive. Here’s how to save on tickets and travel smarter.

Book early, save big

Rail companies release tickets 3–6 months in advance. Early bird fares can be 50–70% cheaper. Set price alerts and buy as soon as your dates are fixed.

Use regional and night trains

Regional trains are slower but much cheaper than high-speed ones. Night trains save you a hotel night and let you wake up in a new city.

Consider a Eurail pass

If you're visiting 3+ countries, a Eurail Global Pass can be worth it. It offers unlimited travel on participating trains for a set number of days.

Compare with buses and flights

Sometimes a FlixBus or a low-cost flight is cheaper than a train. Always compare before booking, especially for long distances.

Travel midweek and off-peak

Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are usually cheaper. Avoid Friday evenings, Sundays, and holiday weekends.
