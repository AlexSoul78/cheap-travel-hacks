How to Find Cheap Flights in 2026

Stop overpaying for airfare. These proven tricks help you find the cheapest tickets and save hundreds of dollars on every trip.

1. Be flexible with your dates
The biggest saving comes from shifting your departure by 2–3 days. Use a price calendar to see which days are cheapest. Midweek flights are almost always cheaper than weekend ones.
2. Use the “Anywhere” search
If you don’t have a fixed destination, search for flights to “Anywhere.” You’ll see a map of the cheapest destinations from your city. This is how you discover deals to places you never considered.
3. Check nearby airports
Flying into a smaller or nearby airport can be much cheaper than the main one. For example, a flight to a secondary city and a short bus ride often saves 30–50%.
4. Book at the right time
For international flights, the best booking window is usually 2–3 months before departure. For domestic flights, 4–6 weeks is often ideal. Avoid last-minute bookings unless you’re ready to overpay.
5. Look for error fares and flash sales
Airlines sometimes publish fares with mistakes or run short sales. Follow deal accounts and apps that alert you when prices drop. If you see a great price, book it immediately.
6. Travel light
Low-cost airlines charge heavily for checked bags. Bring only a carry-on that fits the allowed dimensions. Not only will you save money, but you’ll also move faster.
7. Compare across platforms
Don’t rely on a single search engine. Check a couple of reputable aggregators and the airline’s own website. Sometimes the same ticket costs less directly from the carrier.

Now that you know the tricks, it’s time to search for your next cheap flight.
